Benchmark Automotive Service handles fluid inspections and service at our Missoula shop. We'll explain what we recommend and why before the work moves forward.”

Suggested headings:
- Which fluid service does your vehicle need?

- A symptom may need diagnosis first
- What to bring to your appointment

Transmission FAQ:
“The interval depends on your vehicle and its maintenance schedule. Some manufacturers list different requirements for towing or other operating conditions. Bring any service records you have so the shop can check what's due rather than use one mileage rule for every transmission.”

Brake-fluid FAQ direction:
“Low brake fluid needs investigation. Adding fluid doesn't explain why the level dropped, and changing fluid doesn't by itself repair a braking fault. Tell the shop about warning lights or pedal changes so the next step addresses the actual concern.”

1. Which records, mileage/use details and tests actually determine your recommendation? What do you show the customer?
2. Do you use drain-and-fill, fluid exchange, or both for transmissions, and how do you choose the method for a particular vehicle?
3. A real example when the shop advised against a requested flush or found a leak/other fault instead. No invented vehicle, price or outcome.
