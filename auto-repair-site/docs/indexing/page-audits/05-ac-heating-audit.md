## Ready-to-use direction

Title: Car AC and Heating Repair in Missoula

Opening:
If the fan is running but the air isn't cold, tell us. If the heater barely warms up or the fan only works on high, that's useful to know too. Those problems can need different checks.

Benchmark Automotive Service handles AC, heating and airflow concerns at our Missoula shop. Call or send an appointment request with your vehicle details and a description of what you've noticed.

Suggested sections:
- Is the airflow weak, or is the temperature wrong?
- What to tell us before your visit
- Why adding refrigerant may not solve the problem
- What the service includes
- Request an appointment

Replacement cold-then-warm FAQ:
“Why does my AC start cold and then get warm?”
“That symptom can have more than one cause. Tell us how long it takes, whether the airflow changes and whether it happens while parked or driving. Testing helps determine whether the problem involves the refrigerant system, airflow or controls.”

Replacement recharge FAQ:
“Will a recharge fix my AC?”
“Adding refrigerant does not repair a leak. Before deciding on a recharge, the system needs to be checked and the correct refrigerant identified for your vehicle. Ask what the service includes and whether any repair would be quoted separately.”

Replacement fan FAQ:
“Why does the fan only work on high?”
“The fan controls, wiring or blower components may need attention. That symptom alone doesn't identify which part failed. Let us know which speeds work and whether the problem happens every time.”

Do not add a fixed recharge outcome, universal refrigerant claim or fabricated shop example. A full revised page can follow approval; this audit does not apply it automatically.
