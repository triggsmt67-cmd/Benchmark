## Plan your visit to our Missoula shop

If you're fitting a repair visit around work or a trip into Missoula, get in touch before bringing the vehicle over. That gives you a chance to discuss the problem and confirm an appointment.

After-hours drop-off is available. Ask the shop for the current instructions when you arrange your visit. We'll confirm receipt the next business day after an after-hours drop-off.

Submitting the online form requests an appointment. Your visit is confirmed once you've heard back from the shop.

## Tell us what the car is doing

You don't need to know which part failed. A description of the problem gives us a place to start.

Does the steering wheel shake at highway speed? Do the brakes make noise? Maybe the car starts normally most mornings, then won't start after sitting for a few days. Let us know when it happens and whether the problem comes and goes.

If you notice something on the drive between Lolo and Missoula, describe the conditions. Whether you were braking, turning or driving at a steady speed is more useful than trying to name the repair yourself.

## Understand the findings before deciding on repairs

Benchmark's repair process starts with inspecting or testing the problem. We explain what we find and discuss the recommended work before repairs move forward.

You can ask what the repair addresses and what still needs investigation. An intermittent problem may need more information, especially if it isn't happening when the vehicle reaches the shop.
