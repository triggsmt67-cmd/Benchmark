## Priority fixes / claim ledger

Sources accessed October23,2026. Unverified does not automatically mean false.

1. P0: distinguish maintenance reminder from low oil pressure. The current symptom list includes low level/noise but lacks the warning distinction. Ford owner guidance says to stop safely and switch off for low oil pressure. Do not suggest booking a routine oil change or driving to the shop addresses an active pressure warning. High confidence; low effort.
2. P1: “One late change isn't catastrophic.” Cannot guarantee outcome without knowing vehicle, oil level, history and conditions. Remove reassurance and predictable sludge/wear claims. High confidence; low effort.
3. P1:7,500–10,000 miles and15% monitor threshold as general advice. Use actual vehicle's monitor/manual and time limits; not all vehicles share thresholds. Do not make sticker-versus-monitor a universal hierarchy independent of service guidance. High confidence; low effort.
4. P1: synthetic protection down to-40F versus conventional-20F. Unsupported universal limits; viscosity grade, formulation and required approvals matter. API references SAE classification and oil performance standards. Omit universal cold limits and the claim that all synthetics share one base-stock behavior. High confidence; low effort.
5. P1: ZDDP nonlinear depletion field tests and tribofilm discussion. No studies or appropriate formulation/application scope inspected. Remove rather than make the consumer page a technical paper. Oil degradation is not a universal additive-first sequence. High confidence; low effort.
6. P1: dark oil proves detergents working; oil cap residue proves contamination. Appearance alone doesn't establish condition/cause. Treat findings as possible reasons for further checks, not a diagnosis or reliable service-age measurement. High confidence; low effort.
7. P1: any saturated filter bypass opens to maintain pressure. Oversimplifies filter/bypass arrangements and operating conditions. Omit unnecessary mechanism. High confidence; low effort.
8. P1: observed higher oil-filter contamination on local gravel vehicles and freeze-thaw additive depletion. No Benchmark records or comparative study inspected. Do not present these as firsthand evidence. Use actual driving information and applicable schedule instead. High confidence; low effort.
9. P2: every dipstick/fluid check available, history retrieval, plug gasket replacement only if deformed. Access and procedures vary. Confirm shop final-check process and follow manufacturer replacement requirements, including one-use seals where applicable. No broad DIY procedure. High confidence; owner-dependent effort.
10. P2: March10 review date, badge and current offer. No actual review confirmation or fresh business-price verification. Do not carry badge/date into researched rewrite as new approval; do not silently change offer price/scope. High confidence; low effort.
