## Proposed wording and structure

Suggested H1: Engine Overheating Diagnosis in Missoula

Proposed safety opening, subject to qualified review:
“If your temperature warning comes on or you see steam, pull over as soon as it's safe. Follow your owner's manual for the warning displayed. Don't remove a radiator or coolant reservoir cap while the system is hot, and stay clear of escaping steam.

A lower temperature reading after a stop doesn't tell you that the problem is fixed. Arrange assistance and discuss how to get the vehicle inspected rather than assuming it's safe to continue.”

Suggested sections:
- Overheating now? Start with safety
- What happened before the warning?
- What an overheating inspection can involve
- Does overheating mean a head-gasket failure?
- Arrange an inspection

Head-gasket FAQ replacement:
“Overheating alone doesn't establish a head-gasket failure. Leaks, coolant circulation, fan operation and other concerns may need checking. The shop can explain which tests are appropriate and what the results do or don't establish before major engine work is recommended.”

Cost FAQ replacement:
“The cost depends on what's wrong and the work needed to repair it. Ask about the diagnostic charge first. Once the shop has findings, discuss the recommended repair and estimate before authorizing the work.”

Incident details: exact warning/message, whether stopped or moving, steam/leak noticed, changes in heater output and recent cooling-system work. Do not direct the reader to open a hot system, recreate overheating or keep driving to gather data. No fixed word-count target or invented local case study.
