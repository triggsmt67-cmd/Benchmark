## Prioritized findings / claim ledger

Sources accessed October23,2026. Unsupported does not mean proven false.

1. P0: grinding/pulsing brakes appear as routine-maintenance symptoms. Distinguish an active braking concern from a service reminder. Don't imply routine maintenance addresses a braking fault or that readers should drive in. Qualified review of retained instructions. High confidence, low effort.
2. P1: “Most major mechanical failures” show weeks/months of warnings; degradation predictable/preventable. No dataset supports frequency or warning window. Replace with maintenance can help identify wear and meet scheduled requirements, without guaranteeing prevention. High confidence, low effort.
3. P1: fluid color/smell/viscosity shows how far overdue. Condition cannot reconstruct service age or replace records/schedule. Use owner's already-confirmed Benchmark approach: levels, estimated miles in use, condition, applicable schedule. High confidence, low effort.
4. P1: one belt drives alternator, steering, water pump and AC on every vehicle. Layout varies, including electric accessories and separate drives. Gates supports EPDM wear may not show cracks, not universal accessory claims. Narrow or remove mechanism. High confidence, low effort.

High confidence in narrowing, medium effort.
