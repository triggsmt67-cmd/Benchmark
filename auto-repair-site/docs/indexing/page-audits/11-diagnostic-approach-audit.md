## Priority fixes / claim ledger

Sources accessed October23,2026. Unverified does not mean proven false.

1. P0: “Bring the vehicle in” for flashing check-engine lights, knocking, ABS/airbag/battery warnings. Do not treat every warning as an ordinary drive-in appointment. Refer to exact manual/message and discuss safe transport; do not promise safe driving or ask customer to recreate dangerous conditions. High confidence, low effort.
2. P1: OBD2 scan retrieves freeze-frame/codes “across all modules.” Generic emissions OBD and enhanced system access are different capabilities; supported modules/data depend on vehicle and equipment. Say scan relevant available systems when appropriate, not universal access. High confidence, low effort.
3. P1: P0420 defined as downstream sensor detecting threshold deviation. Ford describes controller catalyst monitoring using sensor information and applicable monitor conditions. A code alone does not dictate a replacement, but simplified sensor-only definition and broad alternate-cause list should be removed or qualified. High confidence, low effort.
4. P1: post-repair scan proves resolved and won't return. Code absence does not establish completed monitor tests or exclude intermittent faults. Government readiness guidance distinguishes codes and monitor readiness. Explain verification appropriate to fault/vehicle with limits. High confidence, low effort.
5. P1: every root cause conclusively demonstrated before recommendation, alternatives always eliminated. Conflicts with the useful “confirmed/likely/still in progress” passage. Preserve uncertainty and the possibility of additional authorized investigation. High confidence, low effort.
6. P1: “Most diagnostics30–60 minutes.” Owner confirmation needed, independent of confirmed45–60-minute oil-change duration. Do not infer diagnostic time from maintenance. High confidence, owner-dependent effort.
7. P2: diagnostic fee credited to repairs. User previously confirmed that policy in this chat; retain scope, do not invent fee amount or additional-time authorization rules. High confidence, low effort.
8. P2: cold-start “false” codes, summer sensors masking cooling faults, gravel-road harness damage. No shop records or applicable manufacturer evidence inspected. Remove invented local pattern explanation; ask about actual conditions without dismissing codes as false. High confidence, low effort.
9. P2: early bearing wear visible in live data, warning timing, delayed failures remove useful evidence. Not universal. Describe inspection/testing choices rather than what every fault reveals. High confidence, low effort.
10. P2: March16 review date/automatic ASE badge. Actual review scope unverified; don't carry forward to a rewrite as technician approval. ASE public credential definitions do not verify this shop's current credentials or review. High confidence, low effort.
