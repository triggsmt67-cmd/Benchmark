## When do you hear the noise?

A new grinding, clunking or rattling sound can be hard to describe. Start with when you hear it: while braking, turning, going over a bump or starting the car.

Benchmark Automotive Service checks vehicle noises at our Missoula shop. You don't need to name the failed part. Tell us what changed and whether you've noticed any difference in braking, steering or how the car runs.

## A noise doesn't tell you whether it's safe to drive

There isn't a reliable rule that says a clunk can wait a week or a squeal is harmless. Similar sounds can come from different parts, and the sound alone doesn't show how much wear or damage is present.

If braking or steering has changed, or the car feels unstable, stop driving as soon as you can safely pull over and arrange assistance. Follow the instructions for any warning light or message in your owner's manual.

For other new noises, call the shop and describe the symptoms before deciding how to bring the vehicle in. Don't make a special trip to reproduce a noise if you have concerns about driving it.

## What to tell us before an inspection

Think about the last time you heard it:

- Had you just started the car, or had you been driving for a while?
- Were you braking, turning, accelerating or traveling at a steady speed?
- Did the sound change as the vehicle sped up or slowed down?
- Did it seem to come from a wheel, under the hood or underneath the car?
- Did it begin after a repair, a pothole impact or another change?
- Have you noticed a warning light, vibration or change in handling?

You might hear a rattle only on the first start of the day. Someone else may notice a hum that gets louder with speed. Those details help us plan the checks, even if neither description points to one specific part.

A note made after you've parked can help you remember what happened. There's no need to record a video or use your phone while driving.

## What we check

Vehicle noises can involve the brakes, steering, suspension, wheel bearings, engine accessories, exhaust or drivetrain. The inspection depends on the sound you report and the vehicle's condition.

We look for the source before recommending a repair. A road test may help when the vehicle is safe to test, but it isn't the right starting point for every problem. Some concerns need an inspection first.

If the noise isn't present during the visit, we'll explain what we could check and what still needs investigation. A quiet visit doesn't automatically mean there's nothing wrong.

Ask about the charge when you contact us. We'll discuss the work before repairs move forward so you can decide what to authorize.
